Projects

P.01 HackGB 2026 · Industrial Track

SmartFlow: Financial Risk Intelligence iOS App

An AI-assisted iOS app that helps small manufacturing businesses understand their financial health and test risks before they happen. I led the product direction for our team of four and took ownership of all the iOS design, architecture, and development.

  • Built three LLM agents on the OpenAI Responses API with structured JSON outputs: a guided business intake interview, a financial document reader, and an action-plan writer.
  • Developed a what-if simulator that combines 4 cost and revenue drivers with break-even risk thresholds, verified by 9 XCTest unit tests.
  • Built Face ID login with a password fallback. Only a password hash and the API key are stored, in the iOS Keychain, restricted to that device.

SmartFlow Web: Manufacturing Risk Analysis Website

The web version of SmartFlow, built with the same team. It is organized into Overview, Explore Risks, Improve Cash Flow, and Advanced Tools. It adds a Cash Gap Navigator that finds short-term cash shortages and weighs supplier minimum orders and payment terms against business cash targets. The frontend, backend, and data structure are set up, and the features are being built out.

The Wealth App: Personal Finance iOS App

A five-tab personal finance iOS app I built on my own, covering accounts, transactions, net-worth planning, and goals.

  • Engineered an on-device LLM assistant with Apple Foundation Models that grounds its answers in each user's own financial data.
