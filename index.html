Projects

The Wealth App: Personal Finance iOS App

A five-tab personal finance app I built on my own in Swift and SwiftUI (~3,300 lines). It includes account and transaction views, a net-worth projection calculator charted with Swift Charts, debt payoff planning using the avalanche method, and savings goals with progress tracking. Data is saved on the device with Codable and JSON.

A normalized relational database for a library, built in Microsoft SQL Server. It models books, authors, copies, visitors, memberships, loans, languages, and sections with primary and foreign keys, plus queries for memberships, book availability, and visitor activity.
